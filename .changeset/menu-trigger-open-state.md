A `secondary` or `ghost` Button holding its menu or popover open keeps a pressed look: the fill deepens to `--selection` and the icon stays `--primary-text`. Before, the hover state dropped as soon as the pointer moved into the menu, so the trigger of an open menu looked idle. Pressing gives the same fill, as on a CommandButton. A breadcrumb step that opens a menu does the same.
