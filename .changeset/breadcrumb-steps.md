Breadcrumb steps are padded segments that take the selection fill on hover, so the hover target is clear. An icon placed before the text turns primary on hover, and the current page is semibold. BreadcrumbButton is a step that acts instead of navigating, e.g. the trigger for a menu of collapsed steps.

A step's text truncates at about 30 characters (`[--breadcrumb-text-max:50ch]` on Breadcrumb changes it) and shows in full in a tooltip, only when it is cut off.
