Add Tree: an expandable hierarchy (folders, org charts, nested categories) with one tab stop and full keyboard support — arrows, Home/End, `*` to open siblings and type-ahead. Single or multiple selection, controlled or uncontrolled open and selected items, `onAction`, and two row layouts (TreeItemLayout with hover actions, TreeItemPersonaLayout). Rows keep a fixed height and hover never moves them. TreeItemRow and useTreeItem build custom rows, including link rows.
