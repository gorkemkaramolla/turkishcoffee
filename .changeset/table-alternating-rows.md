Table and DataTable take two props for how body rows look. `striped` alternates the rows' background, virtualized rows included; it is off by default. Customize it through the `--table-row-odd` and `--table-row-even` theme tokens; hover and selection keep precedence. `selectionColor` colours selected rows: `neutral` (default) is grey, `primary` the brand's soft tint. Customize them through `--table-row-selected` and `--table-row-selected-primary`.
