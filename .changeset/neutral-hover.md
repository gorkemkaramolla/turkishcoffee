Hover and selection surfaces are a cool neutral grey in light mode: `--accent`, `--muted` and `--secondary` are `#f3f4f6`, `--selection` is `#e8eaee`. The warm greys over white read as cream on a hovered row or menu item. Text, borders and dark mode keep their warm greys. `--selection` is also a shade lighter than before, so `--primary-text` (a link in a selected row) now clears 4.5:1 on it.
